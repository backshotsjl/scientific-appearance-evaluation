# Softmaxxing mapped to dating photos

Looksmaxxer slang is common in user questions. Translate it into photo-craft and reversible presentation. Do not become a surgery brochure.

## Language map

Community word → what you actually score and fix

- hunter eyes / canthal tilt → eye visibility, camera height, brow grooming, tired vs rested lids. A phone pic is not a cephalometric x-ray.
- gonial / jaw → beard line, camera angle (eye level or slightly above), lighting from 45°, neck posture, facial puffiness from sleep/salt/alcohol.
- midface / bigonial → do not measure. Note if a low camera or wide lens fattens the face. Fix angle and distance (arm's length + crop, not a 0.5x selfie).
- hairline / hair density → haircut that frames the face, clean edges, product that shows texture. Lighting from front-side, not a bare bulb above.
- skin / texture → sleep, even light, less alcohol-flush, simple skincare, no heavy filter.
- dimorphism / "more chad" → clothes fit, posture, hair/beard that matches presentation, one composed later shot. Facial masculinity in the literature is mixed for men. Do not treat a sharper jaw as settled science or a moral demand.
- body / V-taper → fitted shirt, shoulders back, one full-body in daylight. Score visible adiposity in the photo, not a BMI you cannot know.
- teeth → one smile where teeth are clean and visible if they already smile that way. Do not invent veneers.

If they use slurs for faces, restate the request in rater language and continue. Do not echo the slur.

## Order of work

1. Slot 1 craft (light, eyes, solo, sharp, crop).
2. Grooming that frames the face this week (haircut, beard edge, brows, shirt collar).
3. Body presentation in one honest photo (posture, fit, daylight).
4. Health-color in the face (sleep, not gray, not lobster).
5. Stop. Do not stack ten defects.

Hardmaxxing (implants, bone work, unprescribed drugs) is out of scope. If they ask, say this skill rates photos and presentation, and point them to a licensed clinician if they want medical advice. No brand list. No "do this surgery next."

## Male-presenting photo defaults

- Camera at eye level or a few centimeters above. Never from the hip.
- Light on both eyes. Catchlights.
- Beard either sharp-edged or clean-shaven. Patchy mid-grow reads unfinished.
- Shirt that shows shoulder line without a gym-mirror flex as slot 1.
- Expression: warm-neutral or small real smile. Dead-serious squint loses trust. Fake grin loses status. Literature conflicts on male smiles — lead with approachable, put one composed shot later. See `references/looks-science.md`.

## Female-presenting photo defaults

- Femininity of features is a large, consistent predictor in face research. Do not hide the face with heavy contour filters that change shape.
- Hair and contrast that frame the eyes.
- Same lighting and solo rules as everyone else.
- Full body later, not only face crops.

## Neutral presentation

Skip dimorphism weighting. Score craft, skin, adiposity, grooming, health-color, symmetry-proxy only.

## What you may say

- "This angle hides the jaw. Reshoot at eye level near a window."
- "Skin looks uneven because of the overhead bulb, not because the face is the problem."
- "Grooming is the highest-ROI change this week. Haircut plus beard line."

## What you may not say

- Numeric bone angles from one selfie.
- Race-targeted feature lists.
- "You are a 3, surgery or nothing."
- Crash-cut calories or disordered eating plans.
- Compare them to a named influencer as a body goal.

## After the score

Always pair the number with one executable shot:

Weekend 40-minute list — doorway window portrait, walking mid-shot, hobby in progress, one street full-body.

If they want "mehr Looks", give that list, not a new identity.
