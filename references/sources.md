# Sources

Primary papers the looks / beauty track may cite. Do not cite marketing blogs as if they were these papers.

## Face beauty structure

Rhodes, G. (2006). The evolutionary psychology of facial beauty. *Annual Review of Psychology, 57*, 199-226.  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.57.102904.190208  
PMID 16318594

Use for averageness, symmetry, and sexual dimorphism effect-size order. Quote ranges, not fake precision.

## Speed of judgment

Willis, J., and Todorov, A. (2006). First impressions: Making up your mind after a 100-ms exposure to a face. *Psychological Science, 17*(7), 592-598.  
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01750.x  
PMID 16866745

Use to justify why a hidden half-face makes beauty scoring uncertain.

## Dating-site photo vs stated traits

Hitsch, G. J., Hortacsu, A., and Ariely, D. (2010). Matching and sorting in online dating. *American Economic Review, 100*(1), 130-163.  
https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.100.1.130

Use for craft / app outcomes, not for a person's beauty number.

## Expression vs sexual attraction

Tracy, J. L., and Beall, A. T. (2011). Happy guys finish last: The impact of emotion expressions on sexual attraction. *Emotion, 11*(6), 1379-1387.  
https://doi.org/10.1037/a0022902  
PMID 21604870

Use only for expression. Conflicts with many app-smile tips. State the conflict.

## Perceived health as a mediator

Rhodes, G., Yoshikawa, S., Palermo, R., Simmons, L. W., Peters, M., Lee, K., Halberstadt, J., and Crawford, J. R. (2007). Perceived health contributes to the attractiveness of facial symmetry, averageness and sexual dimorphism. *Perception, 36*(8), 1244-1252.

Use when someone treats symmetry as a bone-score. A lot of the symmetry premium is this face looks healthy in this photo.

## Swipe-app behavior (not a beauty paper)

Tyson, G., Perta, V. C., Haddadi, H., and Seto, M. C. (2016). A first look at user activity on Tinder. *IEEE/ACM ASONAM*.

Use only for market asymmetry and fast filtering. Not for a person's attractiveness number.

## What a score in this repo is

The 1-10 beauty figure is a weighted rater heuristic in `looks_machine.py --beauty-only`. It is not a result published in the papers above.
