# Winning swipe profile

What actually decides matches on Tinder-like apps. Use this for audits and "how must a profile look to work" questions.

## Rank of levers

1. Slot 1 photo — readable face, solo, light, sharp. Most people never open the rest.
2. Grid variety — face, body, life, one social proof, one personality closer. Not six selfies.
3. Face/body signal that survives a 1-second glance (health, grooming, posture, clothes fit).
4. Bio hook that invites a reply. Short beats clever-empty.
5. Recency — lead photo should look like meeting this week, not 2019.
6. After the match — opener quality. Profile gets the swipe. Chat gets the date.

Text never rescues a hidden face. A great bio helps after they already paused.

## Tinder crop

The swipe card is a tall rectangle. A square upload gets center-cropped.

- Face centered, headroom above hair, shoulders in frame.
- Do not put the face at the top edge or it crops off.
- Test the center slice before locking slot 1.
- Hinge and Bumble crop differently. Re-check per app.

## Six-slot recipe (use even if they have 9)

1. Solo chest-up or mid-shot. Eyes visible. Daylight or window. Warm-neutral expression. After crop, face still fills the thumb.
2. Full body. Real clothes, real street or room. Posture long. No toilet, no laundry pile, no gym-mirror as the only body shot.
3. Activity that matches the bio. Hands doing a thing. Face still readable.
4. One social photo. User easy to spot (color, position, or later caption). Max one group shot in the whole grid.
5. Personality closer — pet, kitchen, mountain, instrument, ridiculous souvenir. Still them, not only the object.
6. Second strong face at a different angle or golden hour. Not a duplicate of slot 1.

Slots 7-9 are extras. Drop near-duplicates.

## Slot 1 pass/fail

Fail and reshoot if any of these are true:

- Group or "which one are you"
- Sunglasses or hat hiding eyes
- Face smaller than a stamp
- Harsh overhead or backlit silhouette
- Blur, heavy beauty filter, or warped face
- Bathroom mirror as the only lead option
- Photo older than the current hair / weight / beard by a lot

Pass when a stranger can name eye color and mood after half a second.

## Bio that supports the grid

Soft 80-220 characters on Tinder.

- Caption-bio when photos already tell the life.
- One scene + one taste + one reply hook.
- Ban list unless they insist: love to travel, foodie, partner in crime, fluent in sarcasm, ask me anything, I don't take myself too seriously, no drama, the apps are trash.

## What "success" means here

Do not promise match counts. Apps are sex-ratio and city dependent.

Define success as:

- Strangers can identify them in slot 1
- The grid answers "what would a Saturday with this person look like"
- The bio gives one easy first message
- Nothing in the profile contradicts the photos

If they report dead likes after a new lead photo, wait ~48 hours of normal use before swapping again. One variable at a time.

## Audit checklist

Mark each yes / no / missing:

- [ ] Slot 1 solo, eyes, light, sharp, crop-safe
- [ ] One honest full body
- [ ] One activity
- [ ] At most one group shot, and not first
- [ ] No filter that changes bone or skin
- [ ] Bio has a hook, not a resume
- [ ] Photos look like the same person
- [ ] Nothing in the room screams "I did not look at this frame"

Give the first no as the only next step.
